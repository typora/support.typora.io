## Typora is now macOS Tahoe ready

![Screenshot 2025-09-19 at 3.34.35 PM](/media/new-1.12/Screenshot 2025-09-19 at 3.34.35 PM.png)

The Typora icon on macOS is now Tahoe style, with dark mode, and custom colors:

<img src="/media/new-1.12/截屏2025-09-19 15.42.10.png" alt="截屏2025-09-19 15.42.10" style="zoom:50%;" />

The “window style” option is removed on macOS 26, now defaults to “seamless” style.

Context menu and menu bar now follows macOS 26 style, with icons:

<img src="/media/new-1.12/Screenshot 2025-09-19 at 3.46.28 PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2025-09-19 at 3.46.28 PM" style="zoom:50%;" />

We also fixed UI compatibilities under macOS 26.

Last, the “Share” action was added back, not only for macOS 26, but for all macOS versions supported. You can open share by selecting some text or image, and then select “share” menu item in the context menu.

<img src="/media/new-1.12/Screenshot 2025-09-19 at 4.11.55 PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2025-09-19 at 4.11.55 PM" style="zoom:50%;" />
